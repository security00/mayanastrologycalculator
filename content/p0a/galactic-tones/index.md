# The 13 Numbers of the Tzolk'in, and Their Dreamspell "Galactic Tone" Names

Every day in the 260-day Maya count, the Tzolk'in (Chol Q'ij in K'iche'), has a number from 1 to 13 and one of 20 day signs. Both advance together, so a pairing such as 1 Imix comes back only after 260 days. [1] The numbers are traditional. The names many websites give them, such as "Magnetic" or "Cosmic", come from Dreamspell, a modern system begun in 1987. On this site your result shows the traditional number first and the Dreamspell name as a labelled alias. [2][3]

## Number first, sign second

The two cycles run side by side. The numbers go 1 to 13 and start again, "1 following immediately 13". The twenty signs run from Imix to Ajaw and start again. [6] Because 13 and 20 share no common factor, every number meets every sign exactly once in 260 days. [1] So your *number* is not a separate horoscope stacked on top of your sign. It is half of the name of a single day, such as 11 Ik' or 4 Ajaw.

A run of thirteen days, from a 1 to the next 13, is called a *trecena*, a word Spanish chroniclers used for "a group of thirteen". [7] Dreamspell calls the same run a "wavespell", though its runs fall on different dates because it counts differently (see below).

## What the numbers do and don't tell you

The historical record tells us a lot about *how* the numbers were written and used:

- **They were written two ways.** Bars and dots appear in the earliest records, and portrait heads were used as an alternative in carved inscriptions. Morley found bar-and-dot Initial Series dates nearly 200 years earlier than head-variant ones (Morley 1915: 102–103). [5]
- **They named people and places.** K'iche' chronicles record lords called Wuqub' No'j ("Seven Thoughts") and B'elejeb' Kej ("Nine Deer"). Christenson says such names were likely the person's birth day, "a common practice at the time of the Conquest" (Christenson 2007: 246, 275). [9]
- **They are still counted.** Highland daykeepers in Guatemala keep the 260-day count, and its best-known ceremony falls on a number: Waqxaqib' B'atz', 8 B'atz'. [1][10]

What the record does **not** give us is a fixed personality for each number. We have not found a Classic, colonial or ethnographic source that gives "3" or "12" its own character the way modern tone guides do. The keyword sets attached to the numbers ("Purpose / Unify / Attract" for 1, for example) are published by the Foundation for the Law of Time as Dreamspell teachings. [11] Our pages keep the two apart: traditional material first, Dreamspell in its own labelled section.

## About the name "galactic tone"

"Galactic tone" is Dreamspell vocabulary. José and Lloydine Argüelles introduced the Dreamspell system from 1987, and the Dreamspell kit appeared in 1990. [2] It gives each number a name (Magnetic, Lunar, Electric, Self-Existing, Overtone, Rhythmic, Resonant, Galactic, Solar, Planetary, Spectral, Crystal, Cosmic) and uses those names for the months of its 13 Moon calendar. [11][2] We keep the URL /galactic-tones because many readers search with that phrase. That does not make it a Maya term, and one Lake Atitlán culture site points out that this vocabulary does "not come from any Maya language or community". [12]

Dreamspell also counts days differently. It fixes 26 July 1987 as Kin 34, "White Galactic Wizard", and skips every 29 February. [8][2] That is why a Dreamspell calculator and this one usually show different numbers for the same birthday. On 10 October 2026 they differ by five. ## Questions

**Which number is mine?**  
Enter your birth date in the calculator. The number is the first part of your day name, for example the 11 in 11 Ik'. Then open that number's page from the table above.

**Is a higher number better?**  
No. The numbers count days. We have not found a traditional source that ranks them, and our reports do not.

**Why does my number differ from another website?**  
Most often the other site uses Dreamspell, which follows its own anchor date and skips 29 February. A few use the 584285 or 584286 correlation, which shifts results by two or three days. [13] Our method is set out on the [methodology page](/methodology).

**What about the 20 day signs?**  
Each sign has its own page. Start at the [day-sign hub](/day-signs).
