# The 20 Maya Day Signs

*Prepared by the Mayan Astrology Calculator editors · Sources below · Reviewed 10 October 2026 · Calculation: gmt584283-proleptic-gregorian-v1*

> **Quick answer.** The 260-day Maya calendar, called Tzolk'in by scholars and Chol Q'ij by K'iche' daykeepers, combines 20 named days with the numbers 1 to 13. Each day sign returns every 20 days, and each combination, such as 8 B'atz', returns every 260 days. The signs have Yucatec names (Imix, Ik', Ak'bal …) and highland names (Imox, Iq', Aq'ab'al …). Several English labels found online are borrowed from Aztec days or from Dreamspell, a modern system begun in 1987. [1][2][3]

## How the 20 signs work

The 20 day signs run in a fixed order, and the numbers 1 to 13 run alongside them. After 13 the numbers start again at 1 while the signs continue, so 13 Ben is followed by 1 Ix. Every combination comes round once in 13 × 20 = 260 days. [1][2] The count is still kept by Ajq'ijab' (daykeepers) in highland Guatemala. [2] The numbers have their own pages: [The 13 Numbers (Tones)](/galactic-tones).

On this site every date is calculated with the GMT correlation 584283, in the proleptic Gregorian calendar. Other correlations shift results by a day or two. [4][5]

## Two sets of names

The names most often used in English are Yucatec, recorded in colonial Yucatán and spelled here in the revised orthography (K'an, Chikchan, Manik') rather than the 16th-century spelling (Kan, Chicchan, Manik). [1] The highland names come from K'iche' daykeeping, documented in colonial calendars from 1685, 1722 and 1854 and still in use. [3] We also note older and regional spellings, such as Imux, Keej or Kawuq, on each page.

What the Classic-period Maya called most of these days is still uncertain. Many signs are written with a single picture and few phonetic clues. [6] For a few days the glyph and the word clearly agree: Ik' writes "wind", Kaban writes "earth", Ajaw writes "lord". [1][6]

## Year bearers

In the K'iche' calendar, a 365-day year is named after the day that begins it, and only four days can do so: Iq', Kej, E and No'j (Ik', Manik', Eb and Kaban). [3] These four are also the only day signs that can fall on day 0, 5, 10 or 15 of a Haab' month. [1] → [How we calculate](/methodology)

## Traditional count vs Dreamspell

**Dreamspell** is a modern system created by José and Lloydine Argüelles, begun in 1987 and published as a kit in 1990. [9] It renames the 20 days as "solar seals" with colors and keywords (the last column above), fixes 26 July 1987 as "Kin 34, White Galactic Wizard", and skips 29 February. [9][10] As a result, a Dreamspell birthday usually differs from the traditional day, and Dreamspell names are not translations of the Maya names.

This site calculates the traditional count and shows Dreamspell names only as labeled aliases. → [Dreamspell vs traditional Maya calendar](/dreamspell-vs-traditional-maya-calendar)

## Colors, directions and "elements"

- **Direction.** Prehispanic calendars assigned the days to east, north, west and south in turn. Contemporary K'iche' practice instead links four days with the four directions (Aq'ab'al east, Imox north, Kame west, Q'anil south). [11]
- **Color.** Colonial Yucatec texts pair the east with red, north with white, west with black and south with yellow. "Blue" comes from Dreamspell. [8][12]
- **Element.** Some charts, including earlier versions of this site, give each day an "element" such as fire or water. We found no traditional source for this. Where it appears on this site it is labeled as a modern convention.

## FAQ

**What is my Maya day sign?**
The day sign of your birth date in the 260-day count. Enter your birth date in the calculator above.

**Are Maya day signs like zodiac signs?**
No. Each names a day that recurs every 20 days, not a period of the year. [2]

**Why do sites give different meanings for the same sign?**
Some labels come from Aztec day names or from Dreamspell; scholars also disagree about several glyphs. Each page shows which is which.

**Why does another calculator give me a different day sign?**
It may use the Dreamspell count, or a correlation constant other than 584283. [4][5]
