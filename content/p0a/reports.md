## What both reports have in common

- **The same calculation as the free result.** Day signs and numbers come from the traditional count, using the GMT correlation 584283, version *gmt584283-proleptic-gregorian-v1*. [2]
- **Clear labels.** Numbers come first. Dreamspell names such as "Spectral" appear only as labelled modern aliases. [Why that matters →](/dreamspell-vs-traditional-maya-calendar)
- **Reflection, not prediction.** The longer passages are modern reflective writing, so treat them as prompts to test against your own experience. They are not forecasts, and they are not scientific, medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.
- **No subscription.** One payment, one PDF. [1]

## Scope: what these reports are, and aren't

The calculation is traditional and documented on our [methodology page](/methodology). The interpretation is ours: written in English for reflection, drawing on published sources where we can cite them. We are not Maya daykeepers (*ajq'ijab'*), and these reports are not a substitute for a consultation with one. The compatibility score in particular is a modern feature, "not a documented historical Maya matching formula". [2]
